## 5. Recommendation

**Adopt the autonomy model in stages, and treat the current mandate as a reviewable candidate until it is reconciled with exact governance lineage and its controls are demonstrated.**

Stage 1 — autonomous analysis/recordkeeping: source-first review, current-scope reconciliation, candidate briefs and durable register updates.
Stage 2 — autonomous candidate engineering: isolated branch changes, regression test source, reproducible candidate identity and CI execution, with independent checks that do not let the candidate rewrite its own evaluator or gates.
Stage 3 — bounded automation: permit an explicit pre-authorized class of reversible non-semantic candidate operations under least-privilege credentials, logs and rollback.
Stage 4 — any promotion/runtime/release actions remain separate and require the existing CE authority process. Completing the pre-A10 area review is not Runtime Adoption authorization.

Before any formal adoption, reconcile exact source hierarchy/pointer lineage, inspect actual repository rulesets and workflow protections, run and verify the candidate validator tests and ensure its logic cannot mistake area completion for A10 or production permission. Do not merge PR #27 merely because the autonomy proposal itself says to do so.
