## 1. Purpose

This matrix addresses Zero-Point Finding 4 (Voice Boundary). It exists to prevent two opposite errors:

1. softening a hard truth/evidence boundary into a mere style preference; and
2. elevating subjective style guidance or historical V8 prose rules into a new hard normative filter without an adoption decision.

Every user-facing wording remains constrained by the active Product Constitution, Interpretive Canon, Evidence/AI/Output Validation and applicable commercial/privacy rules. This matrix is not itself an authority source and contains no blanket lexical ban.

## 5. Matrix C — flexible editorial style (non-blocking unless it causes a hard-rule violation)

The following are style goals, not independent truth/authority gates:
- clear, composed, respectful, observant and considerate prose;
- concrete verbs, readable rhythm, minimal repetition and useful specificity;
- avoid unnecessary clichés, filler, empty positivity and needless alarm/fatalistic framing;
- preserve meaningful tension/nuance where the evidence supports it;
- avoid shaming or blaming the user;
- do not make every reading sound positive, generic or interchangeable.

Most of these goals are consistent with the owner-accepted high-level direction and the historical V8 Voice Constitution; the complete historical voice document is not established as adopted V1 normative authority. A style flag should become blocking only if actual wording causes a concrete hard-integrity, commercial, privacy or accessibility violation. Aesthetic preference alone is not evidence of a broken CE rule.

## 6. No blanket lexical ban

No inspected current V1 source establishes a universal blacklist of negative-sounding words or requires every output to be positive. A single word with negative connotation is not enough to prove a violation. Classification must consider claim scope, context, modality, certainty, factual support, user-directed blame/shame, manipulative intent and the governing state.

Examples (illustrative test fixtures only, not approved copy):
- “The calculation is unavailable right now” may be the truthful required disclosure during a technical failure; replacing it with “Quiet Sky” would violate state integrity.
- “This could be a difficult period” is not automatically valid or invalid; it requires a permitted underlying claim, correct possibility framing and no unsupported personal diagnosis/causality.
- “This will happen” is unacceptable when only a possibility claim is authorized, even if the words sound confident or comforting.
- “You are not the problem” can still be unsupported personal assurance; positive phrasing is not automatically more truthful.
- “Your mismatch is noted; the reading does not explain it” is better than inventing a post-hoc rescue, where that state/copy is approved by the relevant product/UI contract.

## 7. Proposed negative and positive test suite

These are proposed test scenarios only; they have no official Test Register IDs and have not been executed.

1. **Evidence support:** unsupported astronomical event, aspect, historical fact or personal fact → claim rejected.
2. **Epistemic layer:** geometric fact and possibility language receive distinct oracles; future guarantee → rejected.
3. **Canon miss:** no matching rule → no interpretation; no “best guess” fallback.
4. **Mismatch:** user-reported “not really” or “nothing like my day” → record mismatch, no narrative rescue.
5. **Psychological/hidden-knowledge:** unsupported trait/diagnosis/relationship surveillance → rejected.
6. **Failure state:** timeout/invalid state → never Quiet Sky, never stale result, no editorial note that implies completion.
7. **Manifest escape:** structurally valid output with extra scope, causal claim, certainty escalation or hidden profile → rejected semantically.
8. **Commerce:** pay/credit tier changed while input, evidence, signal, Canon and manifest are constant → truth/qualification/uncertainty invariants unchanged.
9. **Truthful negative disclosure:** a legitimate unavailability/failure message remains permitted; no positivity-rewriting filter may conceal failure.
10. **Style non-blocking:** a harmless negative-connotation token alone does not cause rejection absent an actual hard-rule violation.
11. **Manipulative language:** fear/shame/false-urgency purchase manipulation → reject under the applicable commercial rule once exact normative/test criteria are bound.
12. **Today's Note:** absent/unapproved/wrong-locale item → omit; valid Quiet Sky remains Quiet Sky; Note never enters Tomorrow Check.
13. **Historical provenance:** loading historical V8 voice text must not silently activate it as a current V1 hard gate.
14. **Versioned output:** each released interpretation remains traceable to Canon/rule/manifest versions; a new voice style version cannot silently change semantic authority.

Before these become official tests, map each to the authoritative test-register ID, resolve any duplicate coverage, define deterministic input/output oracles and obtain controlled test-register approval. The historical v1.5 Register contains valuable coverage but its active authoritative binding remains conflicted.
