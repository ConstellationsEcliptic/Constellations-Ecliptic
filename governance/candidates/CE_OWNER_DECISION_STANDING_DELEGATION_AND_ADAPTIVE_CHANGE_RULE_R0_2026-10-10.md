## 1. Decision

The owner approves the complete operational recommendation presented immediately before this record. CE work is to proceed under the standing rule:

**AUTONOMOUS BY DEFAULT; OWNER CONSULTATION AT PROTECTED BOUNDARIES.**

This is a scoped delegation for the CE work described below. It is not an unlimited authorization, a waiver of governing sources, or permission to self-approve a protected transition.

## 2. Standing delegation

Within existing scope, authority, accessible tools and repository permissions, the operator should perform autonomously and source-first:

- research and verification of governing and implementation sources;
- reconciliation, diagnosis, audits and bounded candidate-level changes;
- development and execution of available tests, evidence capture and regression checks;
- dependency and upstream-data evaluation;
- documentation, portable state records and routine follow-through;
- revision of previously approved recommendations when new evidence or a new case changes their correctness, completeness, efficiency or risk.

The operator must not ask the owner to re-approve routine steps already delegated by this mandate. Work should be evidence-led, traceable and proportionate to the actual risk. The operator must report material uncertainty, inaccessible capabilities, incomplete execution and unverified claims honestly.

## 3. Adaptive change

An earlier approval is not a requirement to preserve a recommendation after contrary or materially new evidence appears. When a recommendation needs revision, the operator should:

1. identify the new evidence or case and cite its source;
2. explain which prior recommendation or assumption it affects;
3. record the proposed revision, rationale, consequences and affected scope;
4. continue safe, reversible candidate work that remains within existing authority;
5. avoid representing the change as normatively adopted until the applicable change-control process has been completed.

The operator must not invent owner approval, treat silence as consent, or expand this delegation by implication.

## 4. Owner consultation boundary

Consult the owner before treating a new choice as approved where the choice is material, cannot be derived from authoritative sources or prior owner decisions, and changes or resolves a protected issue such as:

- product promise, foundational product principle, Interpretive Canon meaning or epistemic boundary;
- personal-data purpose, collection, retention, access or other materially different privacy commitment;
- commercial/transaction semantics, consumer rights or a material external commitment;
- a fundamental risk-acceptance choice, trust root or security boundary;
- an owner-reserved authority, Runtime Adoption, production authorization, required dual approval or SEAL decision.

The operator should bring source-backed facts, the exact decision needed, meaningful alternatives and their consequences, and one preferred recommendation. The pause must be narrow: hold the affected decision or transition, not all unrelated work. Continue unrelated work when it is safe, reversible, within scope and does not prejudice the pending decision.

When a material choice is already resolved by an applicable authoritative source or recorded owner disposition, follow that decision and do not reopen it without a genuine conflict, new material evidence or a relevant new case.

## 5. Non-waivable governance boundaries

This delegation does not by itself:

- amend, replace or supersede any authoritative CE Constitution, Canon, contract, test register or other normative artifact;
- establish implementation completeness, an always-running autonomous service, a trusted runner, independent verification, or full test coverage;
- authorize merge, A10, Runtime Adoption, runtime promotion, deployment, production operation or SEAL;
- bypass source-lineage requirements, candidate isolation, risk-proportional review, independent verification, required dual approval, protected controls or fail-closed gates;
- establish any missing technical identity, test oracle, data package, payment-provider eligibility or other evidence fact.

All such actions remain subject to their own applicable requirements and explicit authority.

## 6. Continuity and reporting

The decision, changes, evidence, unresolved findings, current state and next admissible action must be preserved in portable, durable artifacts so work can continue across sessions without relying on conversational memory alone.

Status reports must distinguish source-verified facts, owner-approved direction, proposed candidate work, work actually performed, tests actually run, unresolved evidence and the next action that is admissible under current authority.
