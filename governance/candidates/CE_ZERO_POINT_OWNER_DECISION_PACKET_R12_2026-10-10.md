Owner-originated premise: one new Deep Sky purchase per authenticated account per CE service day. The UTC Gregorian boundary is separately owner-approved and limited in scope (Box 2515555580794).

**C3 correction in R11:** the purchase-cap trigger, recoverability of an order, and the slot effect after a terminal reversal are separate questions. - **Recoverable failure after debit:** recommended PATH-LINKED-RECOVERY keeps the original logical order, debit and service date and retries/replaces without a new debit. It is not an alternative to a terminal remedy; it applies only while the original order can still be safely fulfilled.
- **Unknown / pending outcome:** reconcile the same order. Timeout, missing callback or UTC midnight cannot prove terminal non-delivery, authorize speculative restoration or release a still-unresolved same-date order.
- **Proven terminal non-delivery:** restore the exact reading-Credits debit once, grant no reading entitlement and apply the approved refund/consumer remedy. This is the terminal branch; a linked retry is not a valid remedy once delivery is conclusively impossible.
- **Purchase-cap event candidate:** count the ordinary purchase when the confirmed order is durably associated with its immutable server-derived UTC service date and its one-time reading-Credits debit is authoritatively committed. Reservation is the concurrency guard; accessible fulfillment is a later state. This aligns more closely with the owner-originated purchase-cap premise than a fulfillment-only cap, but the exact transition remains a recommendation rather than an already approved full state machine.
- **Terminal slot effect:** after complete terminal closure and exact restoration, two options remain: retain the committed purchase event against that date, or release the same-day slot only after full reversal. The [Contract Candidate R0](https://github.com/ConstellationsEcliptic/Constellations-Ecliptic/blob/governance/candidates/ce-autonomous-evolution-charter-r0-2026-10-09/governance/candidates/CE_PRE_A10_C3_PURCHASE_CAP_CONTRACT_CANDIDATE_R0_2026-10-10.md) recommends release after full reversal as a product-fairness default; this remains an owner-level disposition, not an approval.
