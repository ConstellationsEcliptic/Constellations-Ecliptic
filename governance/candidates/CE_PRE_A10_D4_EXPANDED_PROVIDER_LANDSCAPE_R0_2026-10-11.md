## 1. Executive answer

There are more options than Dodo, PayPal, Payoneer, Xendit and Midtrans. A broader scan identifies local Indonesia gateways worth a carefully sequenced written prescreen:
1. DOKU
2. Duitku
3. NICEPAY
4. iPaymu
5. Tripay
6. Faspay (lower priority due to explicit policy language concerning misleading / mystical-superstition material)

These are **prescreen candidates, not providers confirmed to accept CE**. The relevant public pages generally do not explicitly name CE's exact astrology + prepaid Credits model. A missing explicit ban is not approval. Since CE's launch jurisdiction/customer market has not been selected, this is a conditional list for a possible Indonesian seller / IDR payment path, not a recommendation to make Indonesia the V1 market.

For a global digital-sales route, Gumroad's current policy is notable: it expressly allows astrology/tarot/birth-chart material sold for insight, reflection or entertainment, including a one-off personalized reading without a concrete promise of the future. But that is **not the same as allowing CE's current prepaid Credits wallet**: Gumroad separately prohibits billing for services to be performed in the future and quasi-cash/stores of value, with relevant restrictions on virtual credits. Therefore Gumroad is a useful example of a platform that can accept certain astrology products but is NOT an established fit for the exact current CE Credits model.

Other reviewed international platforms have direct conflicts:
- 2Checkout/Verifone explicitly lists psychic or astrological readings/consults and fortune tellers in its acceptance-policy exclusions.
- PayPro Global's published prohibited-products list prohibits stored-value cards/tokens, including virtual credits/payment cards.
- Lemon Squeezy's public list prohibits services of any kind and allows primarily digital goods fulfilled through its own service; CE's current online reading service and Credits model are not established eligible.
- FastSpring's public developer docs identify several prohibited sales categories but do not resolve astrology and CE Credits specifically; that uncertainty does not make it approved and it is a lower priority without a written classification.

No provider has been contacted. No provider is selected.

## 2. Candidate matrix — local Indonesian route

### 2.1 DOKU — first written prescreen candidate

Official sources:
- https://www.doku.com/produk/payment-gateway
- https://www.doku.com/
- https://www.doku.com/blog/qris-belum-aktif-double-check-nama-merchant-yang-anda-gunakan

DOKU publicly offers integrated and non-integrated checkout/payment links, API integration, and 45+ methods including cards, bank transfer/virtual accounts, QRIS, e-wallets and other local channels. Its public product page says it serves various business sizes, including UMKM through enterprise, and accepts Personal, Corporate and International Business registrations subject to onboarding. DOKU's Digital Catalog describes support for physical products, digital products and services.

A DOKU public blog explains that merchant QRIS verification uses MCC/business category and the merchant name must accurately describe the service. DOKU also describes KYB assessment and product/business review during onboarding. A public blog post lists some prohibited businesses, but does not establish a complete policy determination for astrology or first-party prepaid Credits.

**Determination: FIRST PRESCREEN PRIORITY, eligibility not established.** Ask DOKU to classify the actual CE offer and Credits. Do not infer approval from digital-goods capabilities, the Personal registration option, or the fact that astrology is not in a short public prohibited-category list.

### 2.2 Duitku — strong local/IDR candidate for prescreen

Official source:
https://www.duitku.com/frequently-asked-questions/

Duitku's official FAQ says a personal merchant can apply using KTP, NPWP and a bank account, subject to its same-name consistency requirements; business-account documents differ by legal form. A live production account is only available after identity and website verification. The website must be public and active, show clear products, contact information and business information consistent with the relevant permissions. Duitku says it processes transactions only in Indonesian rupiah (IDR), and its payment methods cover local Indonesian channels.

The FAQ also notes channel-specific conditions and that some channels (e.g. Indodana PayLater) are for physical products, not digital balances or digital products. That limitation must be observed if choosing channels.

**Determination: FIRST PRESCREEN PRIORITY for an Indonesia/IDR route only.** No explicit astrology approval was established. Ask about the astrology category, non-expiring account-bound Credits and which methods are permitted for digital readings. Do not assume Duitku solves an international-customer/multi-currency path.

### 2.3 NICEPAY — prescreen candidate, with broad norms clause

Official sources:
- https://docs.nicepay.co.id/en/register-as-nicepay-merchant
- https://docs.nicepay.co.id/daftar-sebagai-merchant-nicepay

NICEPAY's merchant registration docs support multiple business types including individual, individual PT and registered corporate forms, with KTP/director identity, NPWP and other documents depending on type. Its public prohibited-business list does not explicitly name astrology, but includes goods/services violating law or public order, or social, religious and moral norms. It separately bars e-wallet products topped up by credit card.

CE Credits are account-bound service-use entitlements, not a general third-party e-wallet. However, the policy could still be relevant to how the provider classifies prepaid digital value, and the norms clause requires a written category response.

**Determination: PRESCREEN CANDIDATE.** Ask NICEPAY to classify CE's actual claim boundaries and service-only Credits. A published registration path is not category approval.

### 2.4 iPaymu — secondary prescreen candidate

Official source:
https://ipaymu.com/en/terms-and-conditions/

iPaymu's publicly available terms say the merchant must be legal, not fictitious and consistent with what was represented during verification. Its public merchant-prohibition list covers unlawful/defamatory content, illegal or unauthorized trading/forex, self-transactions and security abuse. The page does not expressly name astrology in the reviewed wording, but the limited public list does not establish that the product is accepted, nor does it settle stored-value/Credits treatment.

**Determination: SECONDARY PRESCREEN CANDIDATE due to insufficient specificity of public policies.** Require a written provider category classification before account approval or integration is treated as available.

### 2.5 Tripay — possible small/local prescreen only

Official source:
https://docs-internal.tripay.co.id/en/guide/merchant/registration

Tripay's merchant registration docs require an active, publicly accessible website, a clear description of the products/services and transaction flow, and a reviewable domain. The prohibited categories reviewed do not specifically name astrology, but Tripay warns violations may result in account blocking and permanent balance freezing. The documented URL restrictions exclude some free hosting/subdomain patterns, so a CE custom domain would be needed if using this route.

**Determination: OPTIONAL SECONDARY PRESCREEN.** Do not build an integration based on the absence of an explicit astrology item. Ask for written classification, including Credits treatment and digital-service delivery/refunds.

### 2.6 Faspay — lower priority; material content-category risk

Official source:
https://faspay.co.id/id/ftnc-ops-business-billing/

Faspay's official Business & Billing operating terms contain a negative merchant list that includes content considered dishonest, fraudulent or misleading, with examples that include mystical/superstition material such as charms or objects claimed to have supernatural powers.

This language is not a precise, categorical statement that every astrology service is automatically rejected. But CE is an astrology-based reading product with testable/future-oriented content, so the possibility of categorization within this risk area must be resolved directly.

**Determination: LOWER PRIORITY / MATERIAL CATEGORY RISK.** Seek written pre-screen only after higher-priority providers if needed; no assumption of approval or automatic rejection from the example alone.

## 3. International/platform routes — current model fit

### 3.1 Gumroad — astrology reflection explicitly allowed; CE Credits not established

Official source:
https://gumroad.com/prohibited (last revised 2026-09-16 at time checked)

Gumroad's updated prohibition page states that its "fortune tellers" restriction covers concrete promises of what will happen, readings framed as medical/legal/financial advice, and open-ended practitioner consultations giving instructions. The policy specifically says it does **not** exclude astrology, tarot, numerology, birth-chart and divination material sold for insight, reflection or entertainment; it also allows one-off personalized readings when there is no concrete promise about what will happen.

This is a clear, product-specific positive finding for a bounded reading sold as reflection/entertainment, and it is more precise than the blanket rejection in many processors. However, the same current policy prohibits:
- billing services, including accepting payment for services to be performed in the future;
- quasi-cash or stores of value; and
- specified forms of virtual credits/value that can be monetized, resold or converted to goods or services.

CE sells prepaid, non-expiring, account-bound Credits to redeem later for CE's own Deep Sky reading. Public Gumroad policy does not conclusively classify CE's exact Credits mechanics, but those rules create a material conflict for the existing prepaid wallet-style model. It would be unsafe to integrate Gumroad as CE's provider on the assumption that the astrology allowance also approves the credits ledger.

**Determination: NOT ESTABLISHED / likely incompatible with current Credits-first model without a written ruling.** Do not change CE to direct one-off readings solely to fit Gumroad without a separately authorized product/business decision. Do not present the current design as eligible unless Gumroad confirms it in writing.

### 3.2 2Checkout / Verifone — direct published conflict

Official source:
https://www.2checkout.com/legal/acceptance/

The published policy explicitly lists both "Fortune Tellers" and "Psychic or astrological readings/consults" in prohibited categories.

**Determination: NOT SUITABLE for the present CE astrology reading model on the published acceptance policy**, unless Verifone supplies an authoritative written exception or a materially different policy applies to the exact service. Do not integrate pending such a resolution.

### 3.3 PayPro Global — Credits conflict

Official source:
https://docs.payproglobal.com/documents/legal/prohibitedProducts.pdf

Its published prohibited-products list includes stored-value cards and tokens, including virtual credit and payment cards. This is a material conflict with CE's prepaid Credits, even though the list reviewed does not itself expressly name astrology.

**Determination: NOT SUITABLE for current Credits-first model on present public evidence; no integration.**

### 3.4 Lemon Squeezy — service classification conflict

Official sources:
- https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/prohibited-products
- https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/activate-your-store

Its public prohibited-product page says it generally allows digital goods fulfilled through Lemon Squeezy, but prohibits services of any kind. Its store-activation page says it typically does not approve services or goods fulfilled outside its platform. CE's personal reading workflow is an interactive online service, and the exact Credits model is not established eligible.

**Determination: NOT SUITABLE AS A DEFAULT FOR THE CURRENT INTERACTIVE SERVICE unless Lemon Squeezy explicitly confirms the exact architecture qualifies as a digital product under its rules.**

### 3.5 FastSpring — unresolved, low priority

Official source:
https://developer.fastspring.com/docs/manage-your-products

The public developer documentation lists some prohibited sales categories but does not, in the reviewed page, resolve astrology, future-oriented readings or CE's account-bound prepaid Credits. Digital product/software capabilities do not establish eligibility for a restricted content category.

**Determination: UNCLASSIFIED / LOW PRIORITY written prescreen only**, if the project later requires an international digital-commerce candidate and higher-priority pathways fail. No provider approval inferred.

## 4. Ranked next steps under the standing CE mandate

The recommendation is to avoid expensive integration before policy qualification. If a domestic Indonesia/IDR market is ultimately chosen through the proper project decision, the prescreen sequence should be:

1. **DOKU and Duitku** in parallel: best next policy questions based on breadth of local payment options and documented personal/business onboarding routes; no product eligibility proven.
2. **NICEPAY** in parallel or next: public onboarding supports individual and company categories, but the broad norms restriction must be resolved.
3. **iPaymu** and then **Tripay** as secondary alternatives if DOKU/Duitku/NICEPAY cannot accept the exact model.
4. **Faspay** only with awareness of its higher material content-classification risk.
5. **Xendit and Midtrans** remain in the existing shortlist for written prescreen; each has unresolved/existing category concerns.
6. For a global MoR path, **do not adopt Gumroad for Credits**, and do not shortlist 2Checkout, PayPro Global or Lemon Squeezy for the present model on current public evidence. FastSpring needs explicit classification and is lower priority.

This sequence is only an evidence-gathering order. It does not establish that Indonesia should be CE's first customer market, select a seller jurisdiction, authorize an entity formation, choose a currency, or authorize checkout implementation.

## 5. Standard prescreen request — must match CE truthfully

For each shortlisted provider, disclose in consistent language:
- CE sells astrology-based personal readings grounded in computed celestial inputs.
- Look Today may provide a qualified, testable sky signal; Look Future describes possibilities, not guarantees.
- CE does not claim astrology is scientifically proven, guarantee accuracy/outcomes, fabricate celestial facts, or provide medical/legal/financial advice.
- Credits are purchased in advance, account-bound, non-transferable, non-expiring and redeemable only for CE's own Deep Sky readings; there is no cash-out or third-party merchant redemption.
- State how reading delivery, rereads, idempotency, cancellations, refunds, proven terminal non-delivery, exactly-once Credit restoration, and unspent Credits at account deletion are intended to work; any unresolved consumer-remedy policy must be labelled unresolved.
- State the actual legal seller and customer countries/currency only after those are legitimately selected.

Ask the provider to cite the exact policy clauses and respond separately to (a) category eligibility for astrology, (b) future-oriented/testable reading language, (c) prepaid non-expiring first-party Credits, (d) allowed entity/docs/geographies/payment channels/currencies, and (e) refunds, disputes and settlement. Do not hide the category or describe Credits as something they are not.
