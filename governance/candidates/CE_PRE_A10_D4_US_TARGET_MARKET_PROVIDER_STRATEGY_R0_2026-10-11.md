## 1. Critical distinction: US customers are not the same as a US seller entity

This strategy is for accepting purchases primarily from US customers, with CE's prices presented/charged in USD. It **does not** assume CE must be incorporated in the United States. Payment providers decide separately:
- where the seller/merchant must be established and which beneficial owners/directors they will onboard;
- whether that seller can accept cards issued to US customers;
- whether transactions can be presented/charged in USD;
- whether payouts can be settled into the seller's eligible bank/corporate account and in which currency;
- whether the exact CE astrology service and prepaid Credits are acceptable.

No US incorporation, offshore entity, local director, bank account or tax structure is recommended or authorized merely to access a processor. First establish provider/product eligibility for the real proposed seller circumstances, then evaluate any genuine legal-entity options and their full cost/compliance duties.

## 2. Must-pass CE product classification

CE sells astrology-based personal readings using computed celestial inputs. Its approved integrity boundaries prohibit fabricated celestial facts, invented events/aspects, claims of scientific proof, false predictive accuracy and guaranteed outcomes. Look Today can include a testable sky signal; Look Future presents possibilities, not guarantees. The latter is still future-oriented and must be disclosed in underwriting.

Credits are bought in advance; account-bound, non-transferable, non-expiring, not cashable out; and redeemable only for CE's own Deep Sky reading. Existing paid entitlement rereads do not debit again. Credits are restored exactly once only after the selected terminal-non-delivery conditions are proven.

A provider that accepts US customers, USD, or a generic “digital product” has not thereby approved CE. The provider must classify both (1) the exact astrology / future-oriented product language and (2) the purchased Credits model. Do not hide the category or alter the CE Constitution to pass underwriting.

## 4. US customer policy conflicts / deprioritized options

- **PayPal APM Indonesia:** its Indonesia-specific APM terms list psychic or fortune-teller services as prohibited for the covered APM function. An alternative PayPal wallet-only configuration is not proven eligible; require separate written classification.
- **Paddle:** its published AUP names horoscopes/fortune-telling and store credit/stored value as disallowed categories.
- **2Checkout / Verifone:** acceptance policy names fortune tellers and psychic or astrological readings/consultations in prohibited categories.
- **PayPro Global:** its prohibited-products list includes stored-value cards/tokens and virtual credits.
- **Lemon Squeezy:** its public policy prohibits services of any kind, making CE's interactive personalized reading a poor fit.
- **Gumroad:** explicit allowance for astrology reflection/entertainment and one-off readings does not establish compatibility with CE prepaid non-expiring Credits; it separately restricts future-service billing and stored value.
- **Payoneer Checkout:** its current eligibility note is aimed at US/Hong Kong entities and higher monthly store volume; it is not a low-cost, immediate solution for an unqualified seller simply because the customers are in the US.
- **Local Indonesia/IDR gateways:** DOKU, Duitku, NICEPAY, iPaymu, Tripay, Faspay, Xendit and Midtrans are not the primary research track for the stated US market. Keep them as secondary options only if a specific local method or IDR/domestic path is separately needed. A local acquiring account might still support US-issued cards, but that must be explicitly confirmed and compared on conversion, fees and settlement; do not assume that it does.

## 6. Acceptance packet required for every provider

Disclose:
- Target customers: primarily US; consumer prices presented/charged in USD.
- Actual seller: current real legal status, residence/incorporation country and beneficial ownership when known. These details are intentionally not inferred or selected in this document.
- Service: astrology-based personalized readings derived from computed celestial input; Look Today may produce a qualifying testable signal and Look Future presents possibilities without guarantees. Do not assert “no predictions” if sample outputs do not satisfy the provider's definition.
- Claim limits: CE does not claim proven scientific truth, guaranteed accuracy/outcomes, invent celestial facts or supply medical/legal/investment advice.
- Credits: prepaid, account-bound, non-transferable, non-expiring service-use units redeemable only for CE's own Deep Sky reading; no cash-out or third-party merchant redemption. Explain debit/idempotency, reread entitlement, refund/cancellation, terminal non-delivery restoration and unspent Credits on account closure; identify unresolved consumer-remedy policies as unresolved.
- Ask the provider to cite the governing policy section, identify its actual acquirer and contracting entity, list permitted/blocked payment methods and customer countries, and provide the full fee/reserve/settlement schedule.
