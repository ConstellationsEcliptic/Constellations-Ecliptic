## 1. Why this document exists

The owner has delegated broad operating autonomy for CE work. The operator is expected to handle research, source reconciliation, engineering, testing, diagnosis, documentation, candidate preparation, and routine follow-through without asking the owner to direct every technical step.

The owner should ordinarily receive a synthesized report, a preferred recommendation, supporting evidence, material risks, and only those decisions that genuinely require owner authority.

This mandate records an operating proposal. It does not by itself change formal CE governance or create credentials, background execution, or release authority.

## 2. Constitutional anchor

All work must preserve the active CE normative and governance sources, their hierarchy, and the scope of recorded decisions.

Core invariant:
> CE must never make the user believe CE knows more than CE actually knows.

Related non-negotiable boundaries:
- Astronomy, geometric derivation, qualified signal, approved Canon interpretation, possibility, and user-reported experience remain distinct evidence classes.
- No fabricated astronomy, events, evidence, psychology, personal truth, Canon rules, claim authorization, certainty, predictive accuracy, or social proof.
- A technical/invalid/incomplete failure must not be represented as Quiet Sky, a valid signal, or stale output.
- AI is bounded language infrastructure; it cannot rewrite evidence, qualification, Canon, or an Allowed Claim Manifest.
- Credits and commercial state cannot change calculation, qualification, interpretation, or certainty.
- Current V1 exclusions stay excluded unless the proper change-control path reopens them.
- Historical, working, or conveniently located files are not automatically authoritative.
- A candidate must not change its own tests, evaluator, trust root, or release gate merely to make itself pass.

## 3. Operating autonomy granted

Subject to actual access and connector capability, the operator is expected to:
1. Search relevant Box, Library, Dropbox, GitHub, repository files, and authoritative external sources.
2. Read governing clauses, owner dispositions, supersession records, source lineage, contracts, code, tests, and runtime evidence before material recommendations.
3. Independently diagnose blockers and pursue safe resolutions instead of immediately returning routine work to the owner.
4. Create isolated candidate changes, write regression tests, run available checks, inspect failures, and preserve exact evidence.
5. Reconstruct working artifacts, inventories, handoffs, and audit tools while preserving authoritative originals.
6. Correct prior errors plainly and avoid repeating unsuccessful searches without a materially new hypothesis.
7. Recommend one preferred option when evidence supports it, with rationale, consequences, alternatives, and limitations.
8. Keep a portable record of current state, decisions, findings, exact candidate identity, tests, unresolved issues, and next safe action.

A failed connector action does not prove that content is absent. Unavailable access or unexecuted tests must be reported honestly.

## 4. Change classes

### A — Proceed autonomously

Examples: research, read-only reconciliation, source maps, inventories, diagnostics, test design, regression tests, documentation, portable handoffs, non-semantic refactors, and reversible candidate work that preserves approved contracts.

Controls: isolate changes; record source and candidate identity; preserve baselines; test; provide rollback; do not merge or release unless separately authorized.

### B — Prepare candidate; discuss before material promotion

Examples: changes to user-facing behavior, voice contract, APIs/schemas, dependencies, commercial transaction semantics, Credits rules, failure/remedy handling, privacy lifecycle, test oracle semantics, reproducibility policy, or any V1 feature boundary.

The operator must perform the investigation, build a candidate or diff when helpful, assess user/product/security/privacy/commercial consequences, and present a preferred recommendation. Do not silently promote materially different semantics.

### C — Protected owner/governance boundary

The operator may investigate and prepare evidence, but must not unilaterally decide or bypass the applicable authority process for:
- Constitution, core product promise, epistemic/interpretive boundaries, or Canon authority;
- new personal-data purposes, persistent personal-context reuse, profiling, or changes to retention/deletion commitments;
- owner-held signing keys, trust roots, signer custody, Source Authority policy, or scope of authority records;
- Runtime Adoption, production authority, full runtime coverage, TZIF runtime identity, production/deployment authorization, required dual approval, or SEAL;
- merges/releases/deployments explicitly blocked by governance;
- material legal-risk acceptance, new spending, or irreversible external actions beyond the explicit mandate.

## 5. Blocker protocol

For each blocker:
1. Identify the exact failed precondition and evidence.
2. Try a safe, reversible resolution within the existing contract.
3. Classify the required change as A, B, or C.
4. Continue autonomously for A; prepare a candidate and discuss before promotion for B; stop at the prescribed decision boundary for C.
5. Report cause, recommendation, trade-offs, remaining uncertainty, and next admissible action.

Never weaken a test, bypass a gate, label failure as success, invent approval, or convert a missing prerequisite into presumed authorization.

## 6. Autonomous evolution loop

Observe → bind exact sources and identities → diagnose → plan → isolated candidate → tests and independent checks → evidence bundle → classify change → promote only if authorized → monitor and rollback when needed.

A candidate-generated test result is not automatically independent verification. A passing suite is meaningful only when its expected-value oracle and the identity of the tested candidate are themselves appropriately established.

## 7. Continuity and records

Keep normative sources, owner decisions, source-authority records, candidates, historical material, and raw evidence clearly separated. Prefer new versioned artifacts over rewriting history. Cross-store copies in Box, Dropbox, GitHub, and Library must not be treated as byte-identical without verification.

Every material report should state: what is verified; what is inferred or proposed; what remains unresolved; what was actually changed/tested; exact references/identity; authority boundary; recommended next action.
