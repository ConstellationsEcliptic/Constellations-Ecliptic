## 1. Corrected decision premise

CE's commercial objective is USD-denominated international sales, not an assumed Indonesia/IDR-first checkout. Therefore provider research must prioritize:
1. eligibility for the exact CE astrology product and prepaid Credits model;
2. USD customer charging and, where needed, USD settlement with global card/customer reach;
3. ability to onboard a genuinely eligible seller without unnecessary foreign-company formation;
4. total cost and cash-flow terms appropriate to early-stage microtransactions (setup/monthly/minimum fees, rolling reserves, chargeback/refund fees, payout timing and termination/hold terms);
5. engineering fit with the existing CE commerce contract.

Buyer-facing denomination, processor transaction/settlement currency, seller jurisdiction, and customer geography are four different questions. Wanting USD does not automatically require US incorporation; neither does USD support mean every seller country or product is eligible. No exclusive customer geography, legal seller, jurisdiction, currency contract or launch market is approved by this research.

## 2. Exact product eligibility remains the key risk

CE's paid offer is an astrology-based personalized digital reading. Current invariants include no fabricated celestial facts, certainty escalation, guaranteed accuracy or fake proof; Look Today may contain a testable signal, while Look Future describes possibilities, not guarantees. The future-oriented component is still something a provider must classify honestly.

Credits are bought in advance, account-bound, non-transferable, non-expiring, not cashable out, and only redeemable for CE's own Deep Sky reading. Rereading an existing paid entitlement is not debited again. Credits are restored exactly once only after the selected terminal-non-delivery conditions are proved.

A provider's acceptance of digital products or astrology alone is insufficient. Each candidate must classify both the actual future-oriented language and the exact Credits ledger. CE must not disguise its category or change its integrity rules to fit a processor.

Official sources:

- https://dodopayments.com/currency/usd

Dodo markets global Merchant-of-Record checkout, multiple currencies and USD support. Indonesia is listed for merchant acceptance, with eligibility based on the government ID used for verification (individual ID; or directors/beneficial owners plus the incorporation country for entities). That is a country/identity eligibility signal, not product approval.

Two material issues remain:
- Its official Merchant Acceptance Policy places Spiritual & Astrology services in a category requiring review: “Entertainment only; no claims or predictions.”
- Its policy restricts products involving stored value. Dodo's general credit-based usage billing feature does not prove that CE's prepaid, account-held, non-expiring Credits are accepted.

CE's Look Future remains future-oriented even though it is possibility-framed. Ask Dodo to classify the exact present product, including Look Today testable signals, Look Future examples, and Credits. **Do not integrate or change core product semantics to fit Dodo.** If Dodo will not approve the product and Credits together, do not treat it as viable for current V1.

### 3.2 Gumroad — astrology reflection allowed within boundaries, but Credits fit is not established

Official source (policy last revised 2026-09-16): https://gumroad.com/prohibited

It allows one-off personalized readings without a concrete promise about what will happen. However, its policy also prohibits billing for services to be performed in the future and restricts credits that can be monetized, resold or converted to goods/services.

**Decision:** useful evidence for certain astrology content, but not an established fit for CE's current prepaid Credits model. Selling only single, directly paid readings would change CE's approved monetization strategy; this research does not authorize such a change.

### 3.3 FastSpring — low-priority written classification only

## 4. Priority 2 — specialist international/high-risk acquiring for astrology

Unlike an MoR, a high-risk merchant account generally means CE remains the seller and remains responsible for its own seller obligations, consumer remedies, tax/VAT analysis and operational dispute handling except where the specific contract states otherwise. These solutions can have higher rates, rolling reserves, legal-entity/business-bank requirements and history/volume minimums. Given CE's current minimal-spend posture, **obtain a written no-cost pre-eligibility decision and full quote before any integration or formation cost.**

### 4.1 Daystar Payments — strongest thematic fit to prescreen first

Official source: https://daystarpayments.com/payment-solutions-for-psychic-services/

Daystar's public page explicitly targets astrologers, tarot readers, psychics and spiritual advisors. It describes international/multi-currency processing through domestic and offshore acquiring options, one-time readings and prepaid minute/packages; it explicitly notes that prepaid packages need appropriate underwriting/disclosure. It claims connections to acquiring banks across four continents.

Public evidence does not establish whether it will onboard an Indonesia-resident founder or intended seller, whether USD charging and settlement are available for that seller, whether CE's non-expiring reading Credits count as an eligible prepaid package, or the total fees/reserve/minimum. Its public page has apparent cross-industry copy reused in some sections; verify the legal contracting company and actual acquiring bank before sending sensitive documents or paying any fee.

**Decision:** first specialist-acquirer prescreen alongside Dodo. Ask for seller-country eligibility, USD presentment/settlement, named acquiring bank, exact Credits classification, setup/monthly/transaction/refund/dispute fees, reserve and release terms, minimum volume, payouts, termination/hold terms, and the complete contract.

### 4.2 QuadraPay — astrology and USD/international advertised, but a reseller/intermediary

Official sources:
- https://quadrapay.com/psychics-merchant-account/
- https://quadrapay.com/international-merchant-accounts/
