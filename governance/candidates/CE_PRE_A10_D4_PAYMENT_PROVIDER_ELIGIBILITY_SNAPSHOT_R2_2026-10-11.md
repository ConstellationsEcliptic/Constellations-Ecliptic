## 2. Provider findings from official current pages

Official pages retrieved October 11 state:

- The dedicated Indonesia payment/currency support page says an Indonesian Stripe account can currently accept/pay in IDR only, does not support cross-border or international transactions, and currently has a limited supported payment-method set (the page identifies Indonesia bank transfer/virtual account plus dashboard-created transactions).

Sources:

It is unavailable until invited/onboarded and is currently IDR-only without cross-border transactions. This is incompatible with any assumed international/global buyer path unless the intended product/market model is changed and correctly decided. No market or currency change is authorized here.

A seller account in another country also cannot be assumed through a nominal foreign address or entity-registration shortcut. Use Stripe's genuine local-entity/banking/identity requirements and obtain provider approval for the exact product if researching another jurisdiction:
https://support.stripe.com/questions/requirements-to-open-a-stripe-account-in-another-country?locale=en-GB

### 2.3 Paddle — material direct public-policy conflict

Paddle's official Acceptable Use Policy/help page lists both:

- virtual currency/stored value, including store credit, gift cards and vouchers,

among disallowed product/service categories. The retrieved page reports a last update of 13 April 2026.

Source: https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/what-am-i-not-allowed-to-sell-on-paddle

CE's intended paid product includes astrology readings and an account-held Credits model. This appears to intersect two categories expressly listed in the current public policy. **R2 classifies Paddle as NOT SUITABLE FOR THE PRESENT V1 MODEL BASED ON PUBLISHED AUP; do not invest in Paddle integration or shortlist it as an eligible default.** Only revisit if the exact current product changes materially or Paddle itself supplies an explicit written determination that the exact described CE offer is eligible. This is a provider-policy fit conclusion, not a claim about product legality.

### 2.4 Midtrans — explicit classification risk requiring written prescreening

Midtrans' official business eligibility page lists services containing dishonest, deceptive or misleading content as non-eligible, with examples including products claiming supernatural knowledge and fraudulent content. It also says business type and payment method may have different requirements and directs merchants to contact Midtrans for review.

Source: https://docs.midtrans.com/docs/can-all-types-of-businesses-accept-payments-through-midtrans

CE's integrity boundary prohibits fabricated certainty, claims of proven astrological truth, guaranteed outcomes and misleading representations. That boundary is important but **does not itself prove Midtrans will classify CE as eligible**: the offer is still explicitly astrology-related, and the provider may classify it under its supernatural-knowledge/fortune-telling category. Conversely, the public excerpt does not establish that every reflective/qualified astrology service is automatically rejected.

**Status:** MATERIAL ELIGIBILITY RISK / WRITTEN PRE-SCREEN REQUIRED. Send the actual product description, representative non-misleading copy, claim limitations, Credits design and customer flow to Midtrans for explicit written category determination before implementation or merchant application is treated as viable.

## 4. Required provider prescreen payload

Use one truthful, consistent packet. Do not optimize the wording to disguise the business category.

- Product: an astrology-based personal reading/interpretation experience grounded in computed celestial inputs, with no claim that astrology is scientifically proven, no promise of accuracy, no guarantees and no medical, legal, investment or financial advice.
- Integrity: the UI separates computed signal eligibility from interpretation; unavailable/invalid results fail closed; no invented aspects/events or fabricated certainty; commercial state cannot change astronomical/Canon facts.
- Credits: prepaid account-bound, non-transferable, non-expiring CE Credits redeemable only for CE's own Deep Sky reading service; no cash-out, no peer-to-peer transfer, no use at unrelated merchants. Explain top-up, reading debit, rereading an existing entitlement, terminal non-delivery restoration, refunds/cancellations and account deletion.
- Delivery: explain when a paid service is considered delivered, how failed/non-delivered requests are detected, support/contact handling, consumer-remedy paths, payment disputes and duplicate/late webhooks.
- Entity and markets: exact genuine legal seller, country of establishment/settlement bank, planned customer countries, currency and channels; all remain unset until lawful entity/market eligibility has been selected.
- Request a written answer naming any policy sections, conditional restrictions, prohibited phrases/claims, required licenses/disclosures, permitted payment rails/currencies/markets, credits/stored-value classification and ongoing monitoring obligations.

No live account application or checkout implementation is required to conduct this classification prescreen.
