## 1. Owner direction and stop condition

The owner has directed that, before proceeding to A10 Runtime Adoption, the assistant and owner must first discuss all previously discussed CE product, architecture, commercial, language, privacy, and governance areas, with the assistant bringing source-based, deeply researched recommendations.

This work is not complete merely because an area was mentioned or a prior handoff exists. Each applicable area must be source-reviewed, reconciled against decisions and current authority, and reported with clear dispositions. Any genuine product/semantic/policy decision is brought to the owner as a focused decision, after analysis.

**Pre-A10 stop condition:** do not advance A10 into implementation/promotion/adoption work until the pre-A10 area review has been completed and the relevant owner discussions and decisions have been recorded. Read-only inspection of existing A10 artifacts is allowed to understand dependencies; no A10 gate or authority state is changed by this plan.

This sequence does not change existing A10 technical/governance prerequisites and does not authorize merge, runtime adoption, deployment, production or SEAL.

## 2. Required review method

For every area:
1. Identify the exact current governing normative sources and clauses.
2. Reconcile approved owner decisions, decision registers, change-control, source lineage, supersession and scope.
3. Inspect implementation contracts/code/tests/runtime evidence where actual behavior is at issue.
4. Keep historical, staged, unsorted and non-authoritative material separate unless its authority link is demonstrated.
5. Classify every material point as VERIFIED, INFERRED, PROPOSAL, HYPOTHESIS, UNRESOLVED or HUMAN DECISION REQUIRED.
6. Present a preferred recommendation with rationale, trade-offs, user impact, integrity/privacy/security/commercial risks, dependencies and evidence that could change it.
7. Record the discussion/disposition durably and update the portable handoff. Do not change normative authority merely by recording a discussion.

A failed search is bounded to the searched locations; it does not prove universal absence. Do not ask the owner to repeat an already-recorded decision. Do not produce substitute policy merely because the current source has a gap.

## 3. Area review matrix

The following scope is derived from the owner’s recent discussions and the CE Zero-Point Source-Bound Matrix R0 (Box 2515023681086). It is a tracking plan, not a new normative source.

### A. Product identity and user experience

**A1. Core product promise and epistemic boundaries**
- Verify the relationship between astronomical fact, geometric derivation, qualified signal, Canon interpretation, future possibility and user-reported experience.
- Preserve “The sky provides the pattern. Your experience provides the context. CE connects the two without pretending they are the same thing.”
- Preserve the invariant: “CE must never make the user believe CE knows more than CE actually knows.”
- Confirm no lower layer may rewrite the authority of a higher layer.

**A2. Look Back, Look Today, Tomorrow Check, Look Future, Quiet Sky**
- Verify the exact product/time model and feedback limitations.
- Preserve the distinction between a valid observation with no qualifying signal (Quiet Sky) and an invalid, incomplete or failed technical state.
- Do not create new signal or alter qualification to fill a presentation gap.
- Feedback remains context/reflection, not proof of astrological truth or predictive accuracy.

**A3. Today’s Note × Quiet Sky**
- Reconcile Implementation Plan references to “TESTABLE SKY SIGNAL + TODAY’S NOTE” against Product Constitution and any approved UI/copy/decision record.
- Discover existing approved origin/purpose/presence behavior first.
- Do not invent editorial copy, assume it is part of a personal reading, or decide whether it appears in Quiet Sky without a source/owner decision where required.

**A4. Product language and consumer experience**
- Review whether the language makes CE attractive, clear, gentle and meaningful without making promises that violate its epistemic boundary.
- Separate legitimate user-experience hypotheses from findings supported by evidence.
- Do not use negative-word avoidance as a reason to conceal real limitations or technical failures.

### B. Interpretive architecture and AI

**B1. Canon Rule Registry**
- Verify current registry lineage, approved populated data, schemas, rule provenance and release binding.
- Do not use historical V8 rule lists, tests, or AI-generated meanings as current Canon authority.
- Empty/missing/unverified Canon permission remains fail-closed.
- Establish what semantic coverage is actually proven before recommending user-facing interpretation scope.

**B2. Three-AI architecture**
- Reconcile historical V8 Claude-primary / GPT-reviewer / Gemini-fallback design against current V1 normative AI specification.
- Current V1 Core uses deterministic language templates; Deep Sky may use one constrained LLM processor.
- Keep 3-AI out of V1 assumptions unless a controlled product/architecture change is proposed and decided.
- If reconsidered, analyze privacy, cost, vendor processing, failure/fallback, evidence provenance, deterministic validation and operational authority first.

**B3. Voice Boundary / non-fatalistic language**
- Find any approved V1 voice or language disposition before drafting a replacement policy.
- Keep evidence integrity, allowed claims, uncertainty and truthful failure language as hard constraints.
- Distinguish threatening/fatalistic/demeaning/unnecessarily discouraging language from honest limitation or failure disclosure.
- Do not promote historical Voice Constitution into current V1 authority without lineage.

**B4. Feedback and anti-confirmation safeguards**
- Verify categorical, session-scoped inputs and balanced choices.
- Preserve no narrative rescue, no confirmation loop, no feedback-to-accuracy conversion, and no hidden psychological profile.
- Review the following-day signal comparison without using mismatch as a trigger to invent explanatory narratives.

### C. Paid product, Credits and transaction semantics

**C1. Deep Sky product contract**
- Preserve Deep Sky as the sole paid core V1 product and “depth of synthesis, not truth/accuracy/guarantee.”
- Verify input/output/entitlement contract, preview/disclosure, validated reading delivery, reread, and failure handling.
- Do not infer currency policy, market scope, legal approval, or price from illustrative example values.

**C2. Credits, ownership, entitlement and uniqueness**
- Reconcile account ownership, no-expiry, consumed/granted Credits idempotency, paid-reading entitlement, reread behavior, deletion, refund/restoration and minimal fulfillment records.
- Ensure commercial state never rewrites calculation, qualification, Canon, uncertainty or the reading’s identity.
- Treat the reading entitlement and payment/fulfillment transaction as related but distinct states.

**C3. One new Deep Sky purchase per account per CE service day**
- Preserve the owner-originated premise: one new Deep Sky purchase per account per service day.
- Preserve the owner-approved UTC Gregorian service-day boundary: 00:00:00 UTC inclusive through next date’s 00:00:00 UTC exclusive.
- Reconcile exact current source lineage before applying any normative redline.
- Distinguish reservation, effective purchase/redemption/debit, fulfillment/accessibility and remedies. Do not silently substitute a fulfillment cap for a purchase cap.
- Analyze concurrency, idempotency, provider timeout/unknown states, pre-debit failures, terminal non-delivery, exact-once Credits restoration, same-date retry, date rollover, late-success/restoration race, post-delivery remedy, reread/top-up exclusions and UTC boundaries.
- Present a preferred remedy recommendation only after source and contract reconciliation. The detailed mechanics are not already owner-approved merely because the cap and UTC boundary are settled.

**C4. Payment and paid-market readiness**
- Review current legal/commercial state without treating the internal US research baseline as nationwide launch approval.
- Keep unresolved Credits classification, market scope, consumer remedies and paid-checkout readiness explicit.
- Outside counsel availability is not a blanket CE development gate; unresolved legal/commercial items must be scoped to the functionality they actually constrain.

### D. Privacy, account and scope boundaries

**D1. Account identity, trusted devices and recovery**
- Verify account-owned identity vs device-as-security-layer, session security, recovery-key mechanics, device limits, account deletion and birth-data versioning.
- Preserve approved recovery boundaries; do not create a support override by implication.

**D2. Persistent Personal Context and user data**
- Preserve exclusion of persistent Personal Context/free text from V1 AI input, and exclusions for hidden profiles, user-level behavior profiles, identity graph, public social functions and third-party personal enrichment.
- Separate retention needed for existing entitlement/legal financial records from re-use for a new personalization/AI purpose.
- Any future change needs explicit purpose, necessity, data model, source, visibility, retention/deletion, processor/security, and owner change-control.

**D3. Share Card**
- Share Card is retired/removed from current V1 and is not an open question.
- Preserve historical artifacts as historical evidence; do not reinstate sharing, social rewards, or engagement mechanics by implication.

**D4. Market, locale and currency**
- Check whether any approved decision establishes English-only/en-US-only, US-first release, USD-only or specific market launch constraints.
- Do not infer these policies from examples, language of documents, or the internal US legal baseline.
- Bring an explicit owner decision only if the existing authorized sources do not settle a material product/market choice.

**E3. Engineering quality and test oracle**
