## 1. Conclusion

Preserve the current V1 Deep Sky promise: it is the sole paid core V1 product and sells **depth of synthesis**, not truth, certainty, accuracy, guaranteed outcomes, hidden personal knowledge or psychological authority. Credits may gate access to a synthesis, but may not change evidence, signal qualification, Canon, uncertainty or user-feedback meaning.

This closes the semantic/product-promise review only. It does not establish that a populated approved Canon registry, an end-to-end Deep Sky runtime, checkout/fulfillment, or paid-market launch is ready.

## 2. Governing sources inspected

- **Product Constitution v1.6.1**, Box 2491704535193, §4.5 and §§10–13: Deep Sky combines already-valid evidence and sells “depth of synthesis”; paid truth/certainty/hidden knowledge/guarantees/psychological authority are excluded. The Product Layer cannot change numerical state, qualification, Canon meaning, uncertainty or Allowed Claim Manifest.
- **Business Model Minimal V1 v1.5**, Box 2485331476456, §§2–7 and §§9–15: Deep Sky is the sole paid V1 product; Credits/direct payment are the monetization model; commercial state cannot change astronomy, signal qualification, Canon, uncertainty or feedback. The example 4 Credits = US$2.99 is configurable—not a USD-only policy.
- **Account, Privacy & Commercial Specification v1.7**, Box 2485319995048, §§10–17: Credits are account-owned and do not expire; a fulfilled paid reading may be reread under its retained entitlement lifecycle; purchase preview must disclose scope, covered period, evidence categories, output type, price and Credits cost; payment/entitlement handling is idempotent and fulfillment records remain minimal and reconcilable.
- **Evidence, AI & Output Validation v1.4**, Box 2485336395859, §§3–13 and 21–23: Core language uses deterministic templates; Deep Sky may use one constrained LLM with structured input/output; claims require approved Canon and Allowed Claim Manifest; validation fails closed on missing evidence or unauthorized claims.
- **Owner disposition — Future Deep Sky Cross-Reading Continuity Deferred**, Box 2515528652667: cross-reading continuity is deferred/out of active scope; prior readings/account history/Persistent Personal Context may not be reintroduced into current V1 AI input by changing the payload format. Rereading an entitled reading is separate from using it as context in a new reading.

## 3. Boundaries that must remain separate

| Contract | Meaning | Must not be confused with |
|---|---|---|
| Product promise | Depth of synthesis over already-authorized evidence | Truth, accuracy, certainty or psychological authority premium |
| Canon permission | Approved, versioned rule + matching Allowed Claim Manifest | Model plausibility or general astrology knowledge |
| Purchase preview | Exact declared product/scope/period/output, price and Credits | A promise that every imaginable interpretation can be generated |
| Reading entitlement | Access and reread under retained lifecycle | Reuse of old reading as AI context |
| Fulfillment | Validated reading made accessible under a reconcilable entitlement state | Payment confirmation alone or unvalidated LLM output |
| Daily cap | One new purchase/account/CE service day is the owner-originated premise | Detailed trigger/remedy mechanics or one-fulfillment-per-day; reviewed separately in C3 |
| Launch readiness | Specification exists | Legal/consumer approval for a specific market |

## 4. Recommendation

**PRESERVE the Deep Sky promise and current V1 exclusions.** Do not broaden Deep Sky into cross-reading memory, paid certainty, psychological profiling or an alternate semantic authority.

Before a real activation can be claimed, the separate implementation/verification work must establish:
1. valid evidence/signal inputs;
2. a populated, approved, versioned Canon rule and matching claim manifest for every interpretive claim;
3. constrained language output and fail-closed semantic validation;
4. purchase preview matches the delivered entitlement and scope;
5. payment, Credits grant, entitlement, validated reading availability and any remedy remain separately identifiable, idempotent and reconcilable;
6. historical reading identity/profile version/manifest provenance remain bound for reread.

Current B1 review did not establish a populated approved Canon rule registry in the inspected A9/source paths. Therefore full interpretive coverage for user-facing Deep Sky remains NOT ESTABLISHED. Do not invent a substitute; omit claims without approved semantics. C2, C3, C4 and E3 cover their own remaining commercial/technical contracts.
