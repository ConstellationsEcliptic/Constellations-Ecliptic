## 4. Recommendation

**Preserve the two-layer model: blocking integrity rules first, flexible editorial style second. Do not implement a blanket negative-word filter or forced-positivity gate.**

### Layer 1 — Blocking integrity
- Check approved claim/Canon/manifest provenance, subject/scope, uncertainty and modality.
- Preserve the actual state: valid signal, valid Quiet Sky, error, unavailable, mismatch or failure.
- Reject fabricated evidence, unsupported personal meaning, narrative rescue, deterministic promises, fear-based claims that misrepresent risk, and commercial manipulation.
- Never conceal a material limitation, mismatch or technical failure to make language sound reassuring.

### Layer 2 — Flexible editorial quality
- Prefer language that is warm, clear, specific, considerate, non-fatalistic and not needlessly alarming.
- Avoid shame, clichés, filler and empty positivity.
- State real limitations and uncertainty plainly but considerately.
- A negative-sounding word alone is not a violation. Evaluate the actual meaning and effect, not a simple lexical list.
- Style classification may assist review but must not override the structured evidence, claim contract or actual state.

### Why this is the preferred path
1. It preserves the owner-accepted voice intent without fabricating optimism.
2. It makes substantive truth and system state enforceable while allowing natural language variation.
3. It avoids false-positive style rejects caused by words like “not available” when accurate disclosure is required.
4. It prevents a warm tone from hiding a no-signal result, a genuine mismatch, a missing Canon rule or a technical failure.
5. It avoids silently promoting historical V8 voice rules into the current V1 normative stack.

## 5. Relationship to Today’s Note

The Note needs its own output class/source/display contract. It must stay separate from personal state, signal qualification, Canon interpretation, prediction, Tomorrow Check and the Allowed Claim Manifest. The owner has already approved the limited possibility of an optional non-personal Note alongside valid Quiet Sky, but has not approved an editorial library, exact UI copy, selection algorithm, schema/code or normative amendment (Owner Disposition Box 2515533522798).

The two topics are therefore related but not interchangeable: the Voice Boundary controls how authorized meaning is expressed; the Today’s Note contract controls what kind of content the Note is, where it comes from and where it may be displayed.

## 6. Tests proposed, not executed

Required candidate coverage should include:
- accurate technical limitation is not rejected merely because it contains negative wording;
- fear/fatalism/urgency/shaming is rejected when it materially misleads or violates approved commercial/claim boundaries;
- failure and unavailable state cannot be rewritten as Quiet Sky or a personal signal;
- mismatch cannot be turned into apparent match by editorial prose;
- missing Canon rule cannot be replaced by a positive/general note;
- paid Credits/purchase state cannot change truth, certainty, claim scope or eligibility;
- style evaluator cannot override structured state/manifest validation.

No tests were executed by this review and no test register was changed.
